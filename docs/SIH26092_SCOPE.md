# Yojantra Scope for SIH 26092

## MVP focus

The SIH 26092 MVP is explicitly optimized for the NSFDC problem: helping eligible Scheduled Caste beneficiaries identify the appropriate concessional credit/education-loan option and the right authorized channel-partner route.

This narrower MVP is a deliberate competition strategy. It does not mean Yojantra must remain limited to NSFDC forever.

## Two-layer product strategy

### Layer A — SIH 26092 core

This is the judge-facing critical path:

`Beneficiary goal → profile → NSFDC scheme matching → financial calculation → channel-partner routing → official application handoff`

Core capabilities:

- Micro Finance Scheme matching
- Term Loan matching
- Educational Loan matching
- Rule-based eligibility
- Financial/EMI calculator
- Location-aware channel-partner locator
- Partner routing based on scheme/category and available official partner-status data
- Multilingual chat/voice assistance
- Evidence and source display
- Mobile + desktop UX

### Layer B — Yojantra opportunity expansion

Keep the broader opportunity system in the architecture, but keep it secondary in the SIH demo.

Government opportunities can include:

- Other Central schemes
- State schemes
- Government services
- Startup/MSME programs
- Scholarships
- Grants
- Loans and credit support
- Skill/employment programs
- Incubation and mentorship programs
- Government procurement/market-access programs

Private/independent opportunities can include curated:

- Grants and competitions
- Incubators/accelerators
- Fellowships
- Jobs/internships
- Learning programs
- Funding/discovery platforms
- Business-support services

## Trust boundary

Government and private opportunities must coexist in the product without being conflated.

Every source carries a source type and verification state. Private opportunities must never be represented as government benefits or government endorsements. Government eligibility remains governed by official evidence and deterministic rules.

## UX strategy

The SIH entry experience should be highly focused. A user can start with a financing goal and reach a recommended NSFDC route quickly. Broader opportunities may appear as a clearly labeled secondary section such as **Other opportunities that may help you**.

Example:

**Primary recommendation**

> NSFDC Term Loan — appears relevant based on your provided profile.

**Other opportunities**

> State startup support — separate government program.
>
> Incubator program — private/institutional ecosystem opportunity.

This preserves the SIH problem focus while demonstrating that Yojantra has a scalable architecture.

## Why keep the broader layer?

1. It gives users alternatives when an NSFDC route is unavailable or not yet suitable.
2. It supports preparation opportunities that can help a beneficiary become application-ready.
3. It creates a natural expansion path after the SIH MVP.
4. It demonstrates that Yojantra is a reusable opportunity-navigation platform rather than a one-off hard-coded demo.

## Judge positioning

Do not lead the presentation with the broad marketplace idea. Lead with the exact SIH 26092 pain point and demonstrate an excellent solution. Then show the broader opportunity layer as the architecture's expansion path.

**MVP:** solve 26092 deeply.

**Platform:** retain the ability to connect relevant government and verified private opportunities without compromising source trust.
