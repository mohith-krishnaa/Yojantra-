# Yojantra Product Positioning

## The problem

A scheme directory is useful, but discovery alone does not solve the user's decision problem. Users often have a goal (study, start a business, find funding, improve a farm, find work) rather than a scheme name. Relevant opportunities can also be spread across government departments, state portals, institutions and curated ecosystem providers.

## What Yojantra is not

Yojantra is not a replacement for official government portals. It is not an authority that creates eligibility rules. It is not a generic AI chatbot that guesses benefits.

## Product thesis

**Yojantra is an opportunity navigator that turns a user's situation and goal into a personalized, evidence-backed path of relevant opportunities and next actions.**

## Differentiators

1. **Goal-first discovery** — users start with what they want to achieve, not a scheme name.
2. **Opportunity graph** — connect schemes, grants, loans, jobs, fellowships, incubators, mentorship, challenges, procurement and other opportunities to goals, user stages and dependencies.
3. **Deterministic eligibility** — official/governed rules remain the authority for eligibility; AI cannot invent decisions.
4. **Action planning** — prioritize what to do now, what to prepare next, and what is blocked by missing requirements.
5. **Evidence and provenance** — every governed decision points to the source and catalogue/rule version used.
6. **Multi-source trust labels** — clearly distinguish official government, government-backed/institutional, verified partner and private discovery sources.
7. **Conversational access** — chat and voice collect/clarify information and explain results, while structured systems perform matching.
8. **Multilingual design** — architecture supports English, Telugu and Hindi initially, without treating translation as eligibility logic.

## Judge-facing answer

If asked, "Doesn't myScheme already do this?":

> Yes. myScheme already provides government scheme discovery and personalized eligibility-oriented discovery, so Yojantra is not trying to replace it. Yojantra focuses on the decision and action layer above fragmented opportunity sources: it starts from a user's goal and situation, connects relevant opportunities across government and curated ecosystem sources, explains eligibility with evidence, identifies missing requirements, and produces a practical next-action path. Official sources remain authoritative.

## Product boundary

Government eligibility must remain based on governed rules and official evidence. Private opportunities are discovery recommendations and must never be presented as government benefits. External application submission remains on the official/provider channel unless an explicit integration is later approved.

## Initial opportunity taxonomy

- Government schemes and benefits
- Government services
- Startup grants and seed programs
- Loans and credit support
- State startup/MSME programs
- Incubators and accelerators
- Mentorship and fellowships
- Challenges and competitions
- Education and scholarships
- Jobs and internships
- Government procurement/market access
- Verified private ecosystem opportunities

## Technical moat to build

The moat should not be "we use AI." AI is commoditized and official portals already use conversational interfaces. The defensible system is the combination of a governed opportunity graph, normalized eligibility criteria, provenance/versioning, profile-to-opportunity matching, dependency-aware action planning and continuous source verification.
